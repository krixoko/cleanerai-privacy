CleanerAI – foreground service demo

This video shows the only foreground service in CleanerAI, which creates smaller copies of photos and videos (foreground service types: data sync and media processing; Android 15 and newer starts it as media processing, Android 10 to 14 as data sync). The user selects videos under Tools, Shrink media and taps Create. A persistent notification with a progress bar and a Cancel action appears. The work continues while the app is in the background; tapping the notification returns to the app. The service ends when the copies are finished or the user cancels, and the notification disappears. The originals are kept.
