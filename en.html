Privacy Policy for CleanerAI As of: October 25, 2026 Controller and contact Alex Ovcinnikov kroxoko@gmail.com Processing on your device CleanerAI scans photos, videos and folders you grant access to. The app reads file names, size, modification date, resolution and, for videos, the duration. For comparison, it calculates checksums and image features. This processing takes place on your device. The app does not upload any files or analysis values. It uses the internet only for advertising (see “Advertising”). Purposes and selection The data is used to find identical or similar files, to show storage usage and to create smaller photo and video copies. You select files yourself before anything is changed. You can restrict or revoke the permissions in Android. Without access, the affected features are limited. For a single video copy, you can use the Android system picker without sharing your entire library. This video is only used during the current app session and is not included in deletion suggestions. If you pick a cloud video, the provider you chose may download the video for Android. Processing by that provider is governed by its privacy policy. Optional features Back up before removing: If you turn this on, CleanerAI saves a copy of the files you selected in a folder you choose yourself (subfolder “CleanerAI-Backup”) before they are removed. The copies stay on your device or with the storage provider of that folder; CleanerAI does not send them anywhere. A cloud provider processes them under its own rules. The copies use storage until you delete them yourself. The backup folder is never scanned. Weekly reminder: If you turn it on, Android asks for the notification permission. After that, about once a week CleanerAI checks on your device how much storage is free and when the last scan took place, and may show a notification. No files are read and no data is sent. You can turn the reminder off at any time or revoke the permission in Android. Widget and statistics: The optional home-screen widget shows the free and total storage of your device and reads only these two numbers. The statistics are calculated from the local history. Local storage The app stores protection rules, settings, the last 500 confirmed operations and a limited analysis cache in private app storage. The history contains time, operation type, file count and total size. For the optional reminder, the app also remembers the time of the last scan and the amount of possible savings found then and, if enabled, your choice of backup folder. The analysis cache contains file identifiers, metadata, checksums and image features, but no image copies. It can be rebuilt with “Scan without cache”. Android may also remove the cache. You can delete the history in the app. “Clear storage” in Android removes settings, rules and local app data. Uninstalling the app removes this data as well. Exported videos in the shared media folder and your original files remain. Files in the Android trash follow Android’s retention period. Advertising (Google AdMob) CleanerAI shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the overview, the tools area, the statistics and the history, and an occasional full-screen ad after a completed cleanup. No ads appear on screens where you select or remove files or grant permissions. To load and show ads the app connects to the internet; it holds the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions for this. Depending on your choice, Google may process data such as the advertising ID, IP address, and device and usage information to serve ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Google is responsible for that processing under https://policies.google.com/privacy. Your files, file names, analysis values and history are never sent to Google. Consent: On first start and wherever required by law (EEA, United Kingdom) the app asks for your decision through Google’s consent platform (UMP). Without consent no ads are requested. You can change the choice in Settings under “Ad privacy choices”. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings at any time. No analytics services CleanerAI has no user account, no analytics or tracking SDKs of its own, no in-app purchases and no servers of its own. Apart from the advertising data described above, the app does not transmit any personal data to the developer or to other companies. Cloud folders of a document provider you chose may be made available over the network under that provider’s own rules; CleanerAI itself does not transmit them to a service of its own. Contact by email If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content of your message. This information is used to handle your request and is deleted afterwards, unless statutory retention obligations apply. The legal basis is handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or the legitimate interest in technical support (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your rights To the extent the developer processes personal data about you, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For requests, use the contact address above. Google Play and Android Google Play, Android and, where applicable, your device manufacturer process data under their own privacy policies. This statement describes processing by CleanerAI. Storage history and app sizes CleanerAI stores up to 180 daily readings of free and total storage. A reading is taken about every twelve hours. Low-storage warnings are optional and off. Only on request does the app open Android usage access settings. With that access, CleanerAI reads storage totals for apps with a launcher icon and stores local comparison values. It does not open private app files. Media copies and review sessions JPEG photos can be exported as smaller copies. Capture dates and location metadata are retained when Android makes them available. Photo and video copies are created locally by a visible foreground service. Originals stay. Swipe decisions from one review session are stored locally; they never trigger deletion.
